Why Dubai

The natural crossroads between Africa and the world

For an African business leader, Dubai is not a distant destination: it is the logical extension of home markets — connected, liquid and built for trade.

You still need the right counterpart on the ground. That is the role of a UAE Africa advisor: knowing both shores, speaking the language of Gulf investors as well as African entrepreneurs, and opening the right doors. For Africa Invest — African investment in the Emirates — we are that relay.
