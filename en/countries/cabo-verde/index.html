What Dubai concretely changes for Cabo Verde

Tourism & hospitality

Tourism and hospitality in Cabo Verde attract a new generation of Gulf investors, and Dubai is where those decisions are made. We present your assets and projects — resorts, licences, land — to the relevant operators and funds, and organise visits both ways.

Diaspora

Cabo Verde's diaspora looks to invest on both sides: back home, and in a stable asset abroad. Dubai answers both — property manageable remotely, simple structures, legible taxation. We support these dual projects with a single point of contact.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Cabo Verde → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai real estate from Cabo Verde?

Dubai's market is open to international buyers: residential, commercial and off-plan. We help you define an objective (rental yield, residence, capital growth), shortlist properties and coordinate visits, checks and procedures through to signature.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Cabo Verde entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Cabo Verde?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I open a bank account in the UAE from Cabo Verde?

Emirati banks require a solid file: a company with real substance, documented activity, a clear origin of funds. We prepare that file upstream, select the banks that fit your profile and accompany you to the meetings — in strict respect of compliance rules.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Cabo Verde?

Atlantic hospitality and cross-border diaspora property — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
