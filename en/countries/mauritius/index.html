What Dubai concretely changes for Mauritius

Dual Africa hub

Between Mauritius's ports and Jebel Ali lies more than a shipping lane: a model. Emirati operators invest in African logistics, and Dubai remains the best re-export base towards your region. We connect shippers, forwarders and port partners on both sides.

Fintech & funds

For a startup from Mauritius, Dubai is the door to the MENA market and Gulf capital: sovereign funds, regional VCs, corporates scouting African innovation. We prepare your deck to Gulf investor standards and organise a real roadshow — meetings, not a contact list.

Dubai's property market is fully open to buyers from Mauritius: residential, commercial and off-plan, in a dollar-pegged currency. Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Mauritius → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai real estate from Mauritius?

Dubai's market is open to international buyers: residential, commercial and off-plan. We help you define an objective (rental yield, residence, capital growth), shortlist properties and coordinate visits, checks and procedures through to signature.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Mauritius entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Mauritius?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I open a bank account in the UAE from Mauritius?

Emirati banks require a solid file: a company with real substance, documented activity, a clear origin of funds. We prepare that file upstream, select the banks that fit your profile and accompany you to the meetings — in strict respect of compliance rules.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Mauritius?

Combining a Mauritian holding with a UAE company — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
