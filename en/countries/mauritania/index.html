What Dubai concretely changes for Mauritania

Mining & offtake

Minerals from Mauritania are of direct interest to traders and industrial groups based in Dubai, who sign offtake contracts and provide export pre-financing. We qualify your production, prepare the documentation and arrange meetings with verified counterparties — never opaque middlemen.

Fisheries & agri

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Mauritania, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

GTA gas

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Mauritania → Dubai

How can I sell gold in Dubai from Mauritania legally?

Only through a documented channel: traced origin, export certificates, identified counterparties and Dubai market compliance standards. Our role is facilitation — sourcing, buyer and refinery introductions, due diligence and compliance coordination. We take part in no anonymous transactions.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Mauritania entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Mauritania?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Mauritania?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Iron, gold and fisheries: offtake contracts with the Gulf — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
