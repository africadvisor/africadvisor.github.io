Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Niger

Agri & livestock

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Niger, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Halal export

The Emirates import most of their food, and Niger's halal supply chain holds a natural card: live cattle, meat, processed products. We work on the prerequisites — recognised halal certification, cold chain, consistency — then open the doors of Emirati importers.

Company structuring

Mainland to address the local market, a free zone for 100% ownership and international reach, a holding to organise your stakes: the right choice depends on your activity in Niger and your flows. We compare, cost, then coordinate incorporation, banking and residency.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Niger → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Niger?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Niger entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Niger?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Niger?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Niger?

Livestock and gum arabic towards Gulf halal markets — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
