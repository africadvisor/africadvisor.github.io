Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Benin

Vehicles & fleets

Dubai is the leading re-export market for premium vehicles into Africa: huge choice, verifiable history, competitive prices. For buyers from Benin — an exceptional personal car or a full fleet — we select, inspect, negotiate and coordinate export through to the arrival port.

Wholesale trade

Dubai concentrates within a few kilometres what an importer or exporter from Benin would take months to assemble elsewhere: global suppliers, trade finance, immediate logistics. We organise your buying missions, qualify suppliers and secure the terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment.

Cotton & agri

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Benin, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Benin → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Benin?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Benin entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Benin?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I buy and export a luxury car from Dubai to Benin?

Dubai offers the region's widest choice of premium vehicles, new and recent, with verifiable history. We select against your brief, have the vehicle inspected, negotiate, then coordinate export: documents, sea or air freight, through to the arrival port.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Benin?

Sourcing vehicles and goods for the Cotonou corridor — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
