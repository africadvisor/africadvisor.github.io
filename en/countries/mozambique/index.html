What Dubai concretely changes for Mozambique

Gas & services

Around Mozambique's energy sector, two movements meet: revenues to structure and diversify from a stable hub, and Emirati groups investing in African energy, from conventional to renewables. We support both — UAE structuring, and introductions to Gulf investors and principals.

Certified rubies

Stones from Mozambique deserve better than a sale to the first middleman. In Dubai, a certified parcel — Kimberley for diamonds, gemmological certificates for coloured stones — reaches international traders and real price competition. We verify, document, then introduce.

Ports & logistics

Between Mozambique's ports and Jebel Ali lies more than a shipping lane: a model. Emirati operators invest in African logistics, and Dubai remains the best re-export base towards your region. We connect shippers, forwarders and port partners on both sides.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Mozambique → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Mozambique?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Mozambique entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Mozambique?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Mozambique?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.
