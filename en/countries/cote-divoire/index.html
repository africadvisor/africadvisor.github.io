What Dubai concretely changes for Côte d'Ivoire

Agri-trading

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Côte d'Ivoire, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Rental real estate

Dubai's property market is fully open to buyers from Côte d'Ivoire: residential, commercial and off-plan, in a dollar-pegged currency. Depending on your objective — rental yield, a family residence, value protection — we shortlist districts and developers, verify every file and coordinate visits, negotiation and signature.

Franchises & retail

Tourism and hospitality in Côte d'Ivoire attract a new generation of Gulf investors, and Dubai is where those decisions are made. We present your assets and projects — resorts, licences, land — to the relevant operators and funds, and organise visits both ways.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Côte d'Ivoire → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai real estate from Côte d'Ivoire?

Dubai's market is open to international buyers: residential, commercial and off-plan. We help you define an objective (rental yield, residence, capital growth), shortlist properties and coordinate visits, checks and procedures through to signature.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Côte d'Ivoire entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Côte d'Ivoire?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Côte d'Ivoire?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Côte d'Ivoire?

Diversifying cocoa and cashew revenues towards Dubai — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
