What Dubai concretely changes for Gabon

Wealth management

For wealth built in Gabon, Dubai offers what is often missing elsewhere: monetary stability, robust holding structures and international managers. We frame the thinking — objectives, horizon, family governance — then arrange introductions to family offices and regulated advisers.

Premium real estate

Dubai's property market is fully open to buyers from Gabon: residential, commercial and off-plan, in a dollar-pegged currency. Depending on your objective — rental yield, a family residence, value protection — we shortlist districts and developers, verify every file and coordinate visits, negotiation and signature.

Golden Visa

UAE residency — notably the 10-year Golden Visa linked to investment — gives families and executives from Gabon a stable base: international schooling, banking, mobility. We point you to the route that fits your case and coordinate the file with licensed providers, never promising an approval that belongs to the authorities alone.

Timber & mining

Minerals from Gabon are of direct interest to traders and industrial groups based in Dubai, who sign offtake contracts and provide export pre-financing. We qualify your production, prepare the documentation and arrange meetings with verified counterparties — never opaque middlemen.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Gabon → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai real estate from Gabon?

Dubai's market is open to international buyers: residential, commercial and off-plan. We help you define an objective (rental yield, residence, capital growth), shortlist properties and coordinate visits, checks and procedures through to signature.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Gabon entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Gabon?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I open a bank account in the UAE from Gabon?

Emirati banks require a solid file: a company with real substance, documented activity, a clear origin of funds. We prepare that file upstream, select the banks that fit your profile and accompany you to the meetings — in strict respect of compliance rules.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Gabon?

Family office and wealth structuring for executives — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
