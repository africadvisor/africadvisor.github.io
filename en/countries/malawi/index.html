What Dubai concretely changes for Malawi

Premium agri

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Malawi, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Local processing

Industry and construction in Malawi find capital, equipment and execution know-how among Emirati partners. We identify the Emirati groups active in your segment, present your projects in the right format and structure partnership or joint-venture discussions.

Gulf buyers

Dubai concentrates within a few kilometres what an importer or exporter from Malawi would take months to assemble elsewhere: global suppliers, trade finance, immediate logistics. We organise your buying missions, qualify suppliers and secure the terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Malawi → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Malawi?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Malawi entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Malawi?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Malawi?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Malawi?

Tea and macadamia towards Gulf tables — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
