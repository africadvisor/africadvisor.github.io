Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Chad

Halal supply chain

The Emirates import most of their food, and Chad's halal supply chain holds a natural card: live cattle, meat, processed products. We work on the prerequisites — recognised halal certification, cold chain, consistency — then open the doors of Emirati importers.

Oil & services

Around Chad's energy sector, two movements meet: revenues to structure and diversify from a stable hub, and Emirati groups investing in African energy, from conventional to renewables. We support both — UAE structuring, and introductions to Gulf investors and principals.

International accounts

Opening a corporate or multi-currency account in the Emirates requires a precise file: substance, documented activity, impeccable compliance. We prepare leaders from Chad for these requirements, select the relevant banks for the profile and support every step — in strict respect of know-your-customer rules.

Gum arabic

Around this axis we mobilise our Dubai network — traders, investors, vetted providers — to turn interest into concrete files, with the same method throughout: qualification, introductions, coordination through to completion.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Chad → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Chad?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Chad entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Chad?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Chad?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Chad?

Halal meat and oil: structuring exports to the UAE — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
