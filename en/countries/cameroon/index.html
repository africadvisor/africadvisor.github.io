Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Cameroon

Import-export

Between Cameroon's ports and Jebel Ali lies more than a shipping lane: a model. Emirati operators invest in African logistics, and Dubai remains the best re-export base towards your region. We connect shippers, forwarders and port partners on both sides.

Timber & cocoa

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Cameroon, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Vehicles & parts

Dubai is the leading re-export market for premium vehicles into Africa: huge choice, verifiable history, competitive prices. For buyers from Cameroon — an exceptional personal car or a full fleet — we select, inspect, negotiate and coordinate export through to the arrival port.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Cameroon → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Cameroon?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Cameroon entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Cameroon?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I buy and export a luxury car from Dubai to Cameroon?

Dubai offers the region's widest choice of premium vehicles, new and recent, with verifiable history. We select against your brief, have the vehicle inspected, negotiate, then coordinate export: documents, sea or air freight, through to the arrival port.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Cameroon?

A bilingual trading hub between Central Africa and Dubai — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
