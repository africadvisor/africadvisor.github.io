Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Ethiopia

Premium coffee

Gulf buyers actively seek quality African agricultural products and pay for consistency, traceability and packaging. For exporters from Ethiopia, we identify serious buyers in Dubai — traders, distributors, purchasing groups — prepare samples and terms, and follow the first shipments through.

Aviation & logistics

Between Ethiopia's ports and Jebel Ali lies more than a shipping lane: a model. Emirati operators invest in African logistics, and Dubai remains the best re-export base towards your region. We connect shippers, forwarders and port partners on both sides.

Textiles & industry

Industry and construction in Ethiopia find capital, equipment and execution know-how among Emirati partners. We identify the Emirati groups active in your segment, present your projects in the right format and structure partnership or joint-venture discussions.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Ethiopia → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Ethiopia?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Ethiopia entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Ethiopia?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Ethiopia?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Ethiopia?

Premium coffee and the Addis Ababa–Dubai logistics corridor — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
