What Dubai concretely changes for Nigeria

Off-plan portfolios

Dubai's property market is fully open to buyers from Nigeria: residential, commercial and off-plan, in a dollar-pegged currency. Depending on your objective — rental yield, a family residence, value protection — we shortlist districts and developers, verify every file and coordinate visits, negotiation and signature.

Gulf fundraising

Gulf capital is genuinely interested in solid African opportunities — infrastructure, agro-industry, energy, consumer. For project owners from Nigeria, we act as translator: formatting to Emirati investor standards, pre-qualification, then targeted introductions and follow-through.

Oil & gas services

Golden Visa

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Nigeria → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai real estate from Nigeria?

Dubai's market is open to international buyers: residential, commercial and off-plan. We help you define an objective (rental yield, residence, capital growth), shortlist properties and coordinate visits, checks and procedures through to signature.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Nigeria entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Nigeria?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I open a bank account in the UAE from Nigeria?

Emirati banks require a solid file: a company with real substance, documented activity, a clear origin of funds. We prepare that file upstream, select the banks that fit your profile and accompany you to the meetings — in strict respect of compliance rules.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Nigeria?

Off-plan real estate, Gulf tech capital and a family Golden Visa — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
