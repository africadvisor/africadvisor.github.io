Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Somalia

Halal livestock

The Emirates import most of their food, and Somalia's halal supply chain holds a natural card: live cattle, meat, processed products. We work on the prerequisites — recognised halal certification, cold chain, consistency — then open the doors of Emirati importers.

Ports & logistics

Between Somalia's ports and Jebel Ali lies more than a shipping lane: a model. Emirati operators invest in African logistics, and Dubai remains the best re-export base towards your region. We connect shippers, forwarders and port partners on both sides.

Diaspora

Somalia's diaspora looks to invest on both sides: back home, and in a stable asset abroad. Dubai answers both — property manageable remotely, simple structures, legible taxation. We support these dual projects with a single point of contact.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Somalia → Dubai

How do I invest in Dubai from Somalia?

It starts with a confidential consultation: we qualify your objective — real estate, equity stakes, trade or company setup — then arrange introductions and coordinate every step in Dubai alongside you.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Somalia entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Somalia?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Somalia?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Somalia?

Halal livestock and Red Sea logistics — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
