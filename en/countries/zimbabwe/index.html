Opportunities in detail

What Dubai concretely changes for Zimbabwe

Capital protection

For wealth built in Zimbabwe, Dubai offers what is often missing elsewhere: monetary stability, robust holding structures and international managers. We frame the thinking — objectives, horizon, family governance — then arrange introductions to family offices and regulated advisers.

Gold & lithium

Dubai is one of the world's leading precious-metals hubs, and sellers from Zimbabwe can reach refineries and institutional buyers — provided the file is impeccable. We structure it end to end: documented origin, export certificates, identified counterparties, secure logistics, then introductions to the right desks.

Safe-haven property

Dubai's property market is fully open to buyers from Zimbabwe: residential, commercial and off-plan, in a dollar-pegged currency. Depending on your objective — rental yield, a family residence, value protection — we shortlist districts and developers, verify every file and coordinate visits, negotiation and signature.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, Zimbabwe → Dubai

How can I sell gold in Dubai from Zimbabwe legally?

Only through a documented channel: traced origin, export certificates, identified counterparties and Dubai market compliance standards. Our role is facilitation — sourcing, buyer and refinery introductions, due diligence and compliance coordination. We take part in no anonymous transactions.

How do I set up a company in Dubai as a Zimbabwe entrepreneur?

Two main routes: Mainland (UAE local market) or a free zone (100% foreign ownership). We compare jurisdictions for your activity, coordinate the file, bank-account opening and, if needed, residency.

Can I obtain UAE residency from Zimbabwe?

Yes, several routes exist depending on your profile: residency linked to a property investment, the 10-year Golden Visa, or an entrepreneur residency attached to your company. We identify the right route and coordinate the file with licensed providers — the final decision always rests with the UAE authorities.

How do I find reliable buyers in Dubai for products from Zimbabwe?

Counterparty risk is trading's number-one risk. We never hand over a mere contact list: we pre-qualify buyers (legal existence, track record, capacity), arrange the meetings and stay involved in negotiating terms — Incoterms, inspection, payment security.

Why Dubai for business leaders from Zimbabwe?

Capital protection, UAE accounts and safe-haven property — that is your market's specific angle. Add Dubai's structural strengths: direct air connectivity, a free-zone ecosystem, access to Gulf capital and world-class trade infrastructure.
